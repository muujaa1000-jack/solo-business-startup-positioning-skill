# Fictional complete positioning example

This is a public-safe fictional composite example. Names, situations, and
evidence are invented for documentation; they do not describe a real person,
business, customer, or market result.

## Interview snapshots

The participant says they have repeatedly organized practical workshops for
neighborhood hobby groups, can turn confusing instructions into checklists, and
can reach local volunteer coordinators. They have limited weekly time and do
not want to run a large community or build software.

After one question per turn, the interview forms three candidates:

| Candidate | Fit | Audience and problem | Small service | Largest unknown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workshop checklist kit | Reuses clear-instruction skill | Volunteer coordinators need repeatable session preparation | A tailored checklist and one review call | Whether coordinators would pay |
| Facilitator practice session | Reuses workshop experience | New facilitators lack a low-pressure rehearsal | A small group practice session | Whether the time burden is acceptable |
| Volunteer handoff template | Reuses organizing experience | Coordinators lose context between shifts | A reusable handoff template | Whether the problem recurs often enough |

The participant explicitly chooses the first candidate: “I choose the workshop
checklist kit first because I can deliver it in a limited time.” This is the
user's decision, not a recommendation presented as fact.

### 当前访谈结论

`已形成一个优先定位假设`：a workshop checklist kit for volunteer
coordinators. The strongest basis is the participant's stated repeated
experience; the largest unknown is willingness to pay. The conclusion is a
`待验证假设`.

### 商业验证交接卡

- **一句话定位假设：** Help volunteer coordinators prepare small workshops
  with a tailored checklist and one review.
- **目标付费者及其具体处境：** A coordinator who is preparing a session and
  wants fewer missed steps; who can pay is unknown.
- **问题和当前替代方式：** The participant reports scattered personal notes;
  the problem's frequency is unverified.
- **最小产品或服务设想：** One tailored checklist and one bounded review.
- **收费、获客和交付假设：** Pricing is unknown; discovery may start through a
  coordinator conversation; delivery is a checklist and review. Each is a
  `待验证假设`.
- **已有依据及信息状态：** Repeated organizing experience and reachable
  coordinators are `用户陈述`; value is `外部推断`.
- **三个最可能改变定位的未知项：** Payment willingness, problem frequency, and
  whether the checklist changes preparation quality.
- **优先检查的一个假设：** First check whether coordinators recognize the
  preparation problem strongly enough to discuss a small paid solution.

This handoff card does not equal market validation. Give it to
`solo-business-validation-skill` to test demand, payment, acquisition, and
delivery economics before any full build or spending decision.
